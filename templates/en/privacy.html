Browsing

The site uses no profiling cookies or tracking advertising. To count visits we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personally identifying data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records IP addresses in its technical logs for security purposes.

Route maps

Route maps load map tiles from OpenTopoMap and the Leaflet library from unpkg.com: these services receive the visitor's IP address in order to deliver the files. They use no profiling cookies.

Newsletter

If you sign up for snow alerts, we process your email address only to send you our messages, on the basis of your consent. Emails are sent via Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France, EU). You can unsubscribe at any time from the link in every email; your data is deleted when you unsubscribe.

Telegram alerts

If you use the @crinaleappennino_bot bot we only keep your Telegram chat ID, your language and the places you choose to follow, to send you the alerts you asked for. The data is stored on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which processes it on our behalf. Send /stop to the bot to delete it all at any time.

Sponsored links

Sponsor links are marked as such and lead to third-party websites with their own privacy policies.
